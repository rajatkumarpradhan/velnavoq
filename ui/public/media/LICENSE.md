Video: silent H.264, 768×432, 15 fps, 8 seconds, yuv420p, faststart. The visual is a decorative liquid-glass study, not a demo or a recording of an actual app build. The JPEG is the static fallback/poster. Playback is explicit opt-in and pauses when the page is hidden or reduced-motion preference is enabled. No video source/network download is set until the person chooses Play motion. Decode failure retains the still image. All workflow and app actions are usable without playing the video.
